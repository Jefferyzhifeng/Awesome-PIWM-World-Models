# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We commit to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus feedback on ideas and evidence.
- Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks.
- Doxxing or privacy violations.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation for violations.
