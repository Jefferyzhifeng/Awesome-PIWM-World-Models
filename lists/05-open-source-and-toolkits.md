# Open Source and Toolkits

> Practical stacks for implementing PIWM-related pipelines.

## World Models and Generative Frameworks

- [DreamerV3](https://github.com/danijar/dreamerv3) - Reference implementation for scalable world-model learning.
- [diffusers](https://github.com/huggingface/diffusers) - Diffusion toolkit used in many medical generation pipelines.
- [MONAI Generative Models](https://github.com/Project-MONAI/GenerativeModels) - Medical-imaging-focused generative codebase.
- [score_sde_pytorch](https://github.com/yang-song/score_sde_pytorch) - Score-based SDE reference implementation.

## Medical Imaging Frameworks

- [MONAI](https://github.com/Project-MONAI/MONAI) - Core medical imaging training/inference framework.
- [nnU-Net](https://github.com/MIC-DKFZ/nnUNet) - Self-configuring segmentation baseline.
- [TorchIO](https://github.com/fepegar/torchio) - Medical imaging preprocessing and augmentation toolkit.
- [NiftyNet](https://github.com/NifTK/NiftyNet) - Early but still useful medical imaging DL framework.

## Physics and Inverse-Problem Tooling

- [ODL](https://github.com/odlgroup/odl) - Operator discretization library for inverse problems.
- [DeepInverse](https://github.com/deepinv/deepinv) - Inverse-problem benchmark and reconstruction toolkit.
- [BART](https://mrirecon.github.io/bart/) - MRI reconstruction and signal processing toolbox.
- [SigPy](https://github.com/mikgroup/sigpy) - MRI/optimization-centric computational framework.

## Foundation Models (Medical)

- [MedSAM](https://github.com/bowang-lab/MedSAM) - Medical adaptation of segment-anything.
- [SAM-Med2D](https://github.com/OpenGVLab/SAM-Med2D) - 2D medical promptable segmentation.
- [LLaVA-Med](https://github.com/microsoft/LLaVA-Med) - Biomedical vision-language assistant.
- [BiomedCLIP](https://github.com/microsoft/BiomedCLIP-PubMedBERT_256-vit_base_patch16_224) - Biomedical image-text representation model.

## Reproducibility and MLOps

- [Weights & Biases](https://wandb.ai/site) - Experiment tracking and ablation comparison.
- [MLflow](https://mlflow.org/) - Model lifecycle and registry.
- [DVC](https://dvc.org/) - Dataset and pipeline versioning for large imaging corpora.
