# Surveys and Roadmaps

> Survey papers and roadmap references aligned with the PIWM narrative.

## Physics-Informed and Medical-Physics Surveys

- [Physics-Informed Machine Learning for Medical Image Analysis: A Systematic Review (Banerjee et al., 2025)](https://doi.org/10.1145/3768158) - Large-scale review of physics-informed methods in medical imaging.
- [Physics-Informed ML for Computational Medical Imaging (Ahmadi et al., 2025)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10462-025-11303-w) - Integrates computational imaging with physics-constrained learning.
- [Physics-Inspired Generative Models in Medical Imaging (Araujo et al., 2025)](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-bioeng-102723-013922) - Survey of physically motivated generative methods.
- [Physical Foundations for Trustworthy Medical Imaging AI (Cobo et al., 2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.02843) - Trustworthiness-centric physical perspective.

## World Models and Foundation-Model Surveys

- [A Path Towards Autonomous Machine Intelligence (LeCun, 2022)](https://openreview.net/forum?id=BZ5a1r-kVsf) - Foundational roadmap for predictive world models.
- [Segment Anything (Kirillov et al., ICCV 2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.02643) - Promptable segmentation foundation-model landmark.
- [BiomedCLIP (Zhang et al., 2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.00915) - Biomedical vision-language pretraining milestone.
- [LLaVA-Med (Li et al., NeurIPS 2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.00890) - Biomedical multimodal assistant framework.

## Foundation and Clinical AI Surveys

- [A Survey on Deep Learning in Medical Image Analysis (Litjens et al., 2017)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.media.2017.07.005) - Broad medical imaging deep-learning baseline survey.
- [Deep Learning in Medical Image Analysis (Shen et al., 2017)](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-bioeng-071516-044442) - Core review of early deep-learning trajectories.
- [Computational Histopathology Survey (Srinidhi et al., 2021)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.media.2020.101813) - Pathology-focused technical overview.
- [Deep-Learning Methods for Parallel MRI Reconstruction (Knoll et al., 2020)](https://doi.org/10.1109/MSP.2019.2949470) - Reconstruction-focused technical review.

## Roadmap Anchors

- [Medical World Model (Yang et al., ICCV 2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.01484) - Early evidence for intervention-oriented medical world simulation.
- [DreamerV3 (Hafner et al., 2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.04104) - Scalable world-model direction for broad generalization.
- [Poseidon (Herde et al., 2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.19101) - Foundation-model trend in physics/operator learning.
