# Datasets and Environments

> Datasets and task environments repeatedly used by papers in the survey.

## Radiology and Reconstruction

- [fastMRI](https://fastmri.med.nyu.edu/) - Large-scale MRI reconstruction benchmark.
- [The Low Dose CT Grand Challenge](https://www.aapm.org/GrandChallenge/LowDoseCT/) - Community challenge for low-dose CT reconstruction.
- [LoDoPaB-CT](https://github.com/jleuschn/lodopab_tech_ref) - Low-dose CT benchmark for inverse problems.
- [NIH ChestX-ray14](https://nihcc.app.box.com/v/ChestXray-NIHCC) - Chest radiograph classification and weak-label learning dataset.
- [MIMIC-CXR](https://physionet.org/content/mimic-cxr/) - Large-scale paired CXR-report corpus for report generation.
- [BraTS](https://www.synapse.org/Synapse:syn25829067/wiki/) - Brain tumor segmentation and longitudinal modeling benchmark.

## Pathology and Histology

- [CAMELYON16](https://camelyon16.grand-challenge.org/) - Metastasis detection benchmark in whole-slide images.
- [CAMELYON17](https://camelyon17.grand-challenge.org/) - Lymph node metastasis detection under domain shift.
- [PANDA](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/prostate-cancer-grade-assessment) - Prostate pathology grading and representation learning.
- [TCGA](https://portal.gdc.cancer.gov/) - Multi-cohort cancer imaging and pathology resource.
- [PAIP Challenges](https://paip2023.grand-challenge.org/) - Pathology segmentation and detection tasks.

## Foundation-Model Pretraining Corpora

- [SA-1B (for SAM)](https://ai.meta.com/datasets/segment-anything/) - Segmentation-scale pretraining corpus.
- [PubMed Central Open Access Subset](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/tools/openftlist/) - Biomedical text-image resource for multimodal pretraining.
- [The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA)](https://portal.gdc.cancer.gov/) - Multi-modal oncology source for pathology/radiology studies.

## Simulation Environments

- [Field II](https://field-ii.dk/) - Ultrasound forward simulation environment.
- [k-Wave](http://www.k-wave.org/) - Acoustic wave propagation simulation environment.
- [BART Phantom and MRI Utilities](https://mrirecon.github.io/bart/) - MRI simulation and reconstruction support tools.
